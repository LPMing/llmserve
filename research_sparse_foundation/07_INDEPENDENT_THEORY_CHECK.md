# 07 — Independent theory and reference-scheduler sanity

## Scope

The sparse Qwen P0/P1 model cannot be independently evaluated without the real
route matrix and rank map. To verify only the generic mathematical plumbing, we
implemented two independent checks:

1. Closed-form combinatorics for the explicitly hypothetical uniform top-k
owner expectation in `05_P0_THEORY.md`.
2. A small progressive-filling, max-min fluid reference scheduler in
   `theory_reference.py`. It accepts flow release times, path link sets, byte
   sizes, and directed-link capacities; it checks that allocated link rates do
   not exceed capacity, and preserves finite-flow byte completion.

These are analytic/small abstract resource checks, **not** a replacement for
FlowSim and not Qwen performance results.

## Reference cases

`reference_scheduler_checks.csv` and `uniform_route_expectation.csv` are
generated by:

| Case | Result | Hand-check |
|---|---|---|
| Two flows, one 10-byte/unit shared cut (KV=100, EP=10) | PASS; EP=2, KV=11 | While both active each gets 5; EP completes at 2, KV drains remaining 90 at rate 10 |
| KV/EP use disjoint 10-byte/unit cuts | PASS; EP=1, KV=10 | No shared resource; isolated serialization times retained |
| Isolated EP=10 on capacity 10 | PASS; EP=1 | `bytes/capacity` lower bound is attained |
| Two flows share a 4-byte/unit narrow cut; one also uses a 10-byte/unit wide cut | PASS; both=4 | Narrow cut limits each to 2; its aggregate rate is exactly 4 |

The combinatorics table checks `1 ≤ U(p) ≤ min(p,8)`, source symmetry, and
nonnegative remote owners at EP `{4,8,16,32,64}`. Exact values are shown in
`05_P0_THEORY.md` and the CSV; all rows are marked
`ANALYTIC_ASSUMPTION_ONLY`.

## What this does and does not establish

It establishes that the generic cut lower-bound and fluid resource accounting
used in this audit are self-consistent on small hand-checkable cases. It does
not establish sparse-A2A compute/communication equivalence, Qwen route
provenance, actual physical path capacity, collective peer barriers, profile
validity at EP32/64, or a serving gain. Those are blocked upstream and are not
filled in with toy data.
